FAQs

Before you book

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly. If you are claiming on insurance, check whether your insurer needs one.

Which insurers do you work with?

Most major UK health insurers. Bring your authorisation code to your first appointment.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable that lets us see and move the area being treated.

Can I cancel or move my appointment?

Yes, in your patient account, free of charge up to 24 hours before.
