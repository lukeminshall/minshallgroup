## About (paste into the Overview)

Minshall Group is a strategy, digital, data and brand group based in Leeds. Clients bring us a number that needs to move: new revenue, recovered margin or unlocked capital. We work out how, build what it takes, and stay until it has moved.

We work as one team across four practices: strategy and consultancy, digital and product, data, AI and automation, and brand, marketing and growth. Most programmes use two or three of them together, so the people who shape the strategy also build the platform and measure the result.

We know health and care, sport, wellbeing, universities, the public sector and growth companies well, and we take overseas health technology companies into the UK alongside XV Capital Advisory.

We also build and run our own products. DataLense is healthcare commercial intelligence for site and investment decisions. Straight Talking gives patients a simple way to say how their care felt, in the moment, and gives health and social care services a direct line back.

For small and medium-sized businesses, we offer fixed-price websites from £495, with a free website check, the Webmins Score.

Minshall Group was formerly Knexu. Minshall Group Limited is registered in England and Wales, company number 17529393.
