Where sport meets health

England is more active than at any point in a decade, but the gains are going to people who were already likely to move. Meanwhile almost half a million adults wait for community MSK care. The opportunity lies in the ground between sport and the NHS.

▪️ 64.6%: Adults in England meeting the CMO activity guideline, Nov 2024 to Nov 2025 (Sport England Active Lives)
▪️ 28.4%: Children in Years 1 to 11 doing less than 30 minutes of activity a day, 2024-25 academic year (Sport England Active Lives CYP)
▪️ 456,532: Adults on community musculoskeletal waiting lists in England, August 2026 (NHS England CHS waiting lists)
