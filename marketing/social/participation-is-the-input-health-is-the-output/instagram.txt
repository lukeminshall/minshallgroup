Participation is the input. Health is the output.

Physical activity is one of the best-evidenced ways to change health. Most sport and activity programmes still report how many people took part, not what changed for them.

▪️ Up to 40%: Lower risk of type 2 diabetes from regular physical activity (UK Chief Medical Officers’ guidelines infographic)
▪️ 1 in 6: Deaths in the UK associated with physical inactivity (OHID, Physical activity: applying All Our Health)
▪️ £7.4bn: Estimated annual cost of physical inactivity to the UK, including £0.9bn to the NHS (OHID)
