Health startups and the health economy

The UK has thousands of small health and life sciences companies and a health service that says it needs them. Their real effect on the health economy depends on whether anything changes for patients and services, not on how much they raise.

▪️ 6,500: Businesses operating in UK life sciences, 2024/25, employing 306,700 people (Office for Life Sciences)
▪️ 88%: Share of UK life sciences companies that are SMEs, 2024/25 (Office for Life Sciences)
▪️ 33%: MedTech firms reporting they had removed products from the UK market in 2024 (Life Sciences Sector Plan, July 2025)
