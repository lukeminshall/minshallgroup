7.4m: Referral to treatment pathways waiting in England at the end of August 2026, around 6.2 million patients (NHS England).

NHS waiting targets count the time to a test or a treatment. What patients wait for is an answer. Measuring that would change how services are designed and judged.

Also in the piece:
• 24.7%: Patients waiting six weeks or more for one of 15 key diagnostic tests, August 2026, against a 1% standard last met in 2013 (NHS England)
• 78.6%: People told whether they had cancer within 28 days of an urgent referral, August 2026, against an 80% standard (NHS England)
• £2.7bn: Minimum extra pay for working-age patients by 2030/31 if elective waits returned to the 18-week standard (NHS England and ONS analysts, 2025)
