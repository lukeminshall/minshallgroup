The business of UK sport, 2026: where the money is moving

Record revenues, record audiences and record activity levels hide a sharper story. Value is shifting towards fans, partners, women’s sport and flexible participation, and it is concentrating as it moves.

▪️ +39%: Growth in Women’s Super League clubs’ revenue to £90m in 2024/25 (Deloitte Annual Review of Football Finance 2026)
