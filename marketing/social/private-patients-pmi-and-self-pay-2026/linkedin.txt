953,000: Private hospital admissions in the UK, 2025 (PHIN).

Private hospital admissions hit a fourth successive record in 2025, but the growth came from insurance while self-pay stood still. The early 2026 data suggests that is shifting again, and the regional pattern matters more than the national totals.
