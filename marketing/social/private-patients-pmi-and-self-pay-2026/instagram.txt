Who pays for private care now, and what it means for where providers build

Private hospital admissions hit a fourth successive record in 2025, but the growth came from insurance while self-pay stood still. The early 2026 data suggests that is shifting again, and the regional pattern matters more than the national totals.

▪️ 953,000: Private hospital admissions in the UK, 2025 (PHIN)
▪️ 282,625: Self-pay admissions in 2025, within 100 of the 2023 peak (PHIN)
▪️ 6.5m: People covered by private medical insurance in 2024, 4.8m of them through work (ABI)
