Sponsorship that counts

Sports sponsorship is a big market that measures itself mostly in logos and impressions. The rules, the buyers and the public are starting to ask what it actually changed.

▪️ $42.0bn: Estimated value of the global sports sponsorship market in 2025, up from $29.7bn in 2020 (GlobalData)
▪️ 37%: Marketers with a standardised process for measuring return on sponsorship, US survey (ANA and MASB, 2018)
▪️ £2.4bn: Commercial revenue of Premier League clubs in 2024/25, up 13% on the year (Deloitte)
