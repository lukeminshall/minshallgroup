Leisure centres are health infrastructure

Public leisure centres prevent illness, support people back from cancer and reach the places with the poorest health. They are still funded and judged as if they were gyms.

▪️ £188m: Estimated health savings in one year from participation at 529 public leisure centres, led by depression and type 2 diabetes, 2024/25 (Sport England, Moving Communities)
▪️ 500: Swimming pools lost since 2010, 42% of them since 2020 (Swim England and ukactive, 2025)
▪️ 56.7%: Adults active in the most deprived places in England, against 69.8% in the least deprived, November 2024 to 2025 (Sport England, Active Lives)
