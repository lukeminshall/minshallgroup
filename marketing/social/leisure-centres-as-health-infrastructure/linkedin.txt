£188m: Estimated health savings in one year from participation at 529 public leisure centres, led by depression and type 2 diabetes, 2024/25 (Sport England, Moving Communities).

Public leisure centres prevent illness, support people back from cancer and reach the places with the poorest health. They are still funded and judged as if they were gyms.
