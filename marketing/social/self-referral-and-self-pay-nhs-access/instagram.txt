Self-referral and self-pay

More patients are walking into care without a GP referral, through NHS self-referral or by paying. Each side has solved part of the access problem the other is still struggling with.

▪️ 16%: Adults in England who used private health services in the past year, up from 9% in 2023 (Healthwatch England and Savanta, 2025 poll)
▪️ 7.7%: Rise in self-pay private hospital admissions in January to March 2026 on a year earlier, to their highest ever level (PHIN)
▪️ 1.81m: Referrals to NHS Talking Therapies in 2025-26, a service anyone can self-refer to without seeing a GP (NHS England)
