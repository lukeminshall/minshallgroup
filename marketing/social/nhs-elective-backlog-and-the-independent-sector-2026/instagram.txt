The elective backlog in 2026

England hit its 65% interim waiting time target in March 2026 and has not moved since. The latest data shows where the long waits are, where independent providers actually work, and why the two do not line up.

▪️ 7.37m: RTT pathways on the waiting list in England, end of August 2026 (about 6.2m individual patients)
▪️ 65.2%: Pathways waiting under 18 weeks, August 2026, against a 92% constitutional standard
▪️ 113,339: Pathways waiting over 52 weeks, August 2026, up from about 94,400 in March 2026
