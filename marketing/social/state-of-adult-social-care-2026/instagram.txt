The state of adult social care in England, 2026

Vacancies are at a ten-year low, but the overseas recruitment that closed the gap has largely stopped, councils are still overspending and hospital discharge delays are rising. Here is what the latest data means for providers and investors.

▪️ 6.2%: Adult social care vacancy rate in 2025/26, the lowest since 2015/16 (Skills for Care)
▪️ 30,000: International recruits starting direct care roles in 2025/26, down from 105,000 in 2023/24 (Skills for Care)
▪️ £715m: Council overspend on adult social care budgets in 2025/26 (ADASS Spring Survey 2026)
