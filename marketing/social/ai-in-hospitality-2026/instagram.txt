AI in hospitality

Hospitality uses AI less than almost any other sector, under more cost pressure than most. The opportunity is real, but only if it is judged on staff time, waste and guests, not on the tool.

▪️ 23%: Hotel and catering businesses using at least one AI technology in early 2026, the lowest of any sector, against 32% of all UK businesses (DSIT)
▪️ 977: Net closures of licensed premises in Great Britain in the year to September 2026, nearly 19 a week (NIQ Hospitality Market Monitor)
▪️ 6.2%: Share of staff in hotels and restaurants not fully proficient in their jobs, the highest skills-gap density of any sector, 2024 (DfE)
