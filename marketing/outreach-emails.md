## Before you send: the rules that keep this legal and out of spam

1. **Who you can cold email (UK PECR).** You may email **limited companies, LLPs, public bodies and other corporate bodies** without prior consent, as long as the email is relevant to their role and has a clear opt-out. You may **not** cold email **sole traders or partnerships** without consent. Many cafés, trades and salons are sole traders. Check Companies House first; if they are not a company, use a letter, a phone call, a LinkedIn message or a walk-in instead.
2. **UK GDPR.** We rely on legitimate interests. Keep a short note of where each address came from (their website, Companies House, a directory). Use role or published addresses where you can. Delete anyone who opts out and never email them again.
3. **Every email must** say who we are, give a postal address and offer an easy opt-out. The footer below does all three.
4. **Deliverability.** Send one-to-one, 20 to 40 a day at first, written to a named person where possible. No attachments, one link, no images. Do not use bought lists. If volume grows, send from a subdomain (news.minshallgroup.com is already verified) so hello@ keeps its reputation.
5. **Run the score first.** For small businesses, use `/tools/prospect` to score their site before emailing. A real score for their own website is the strongest opening line we have.
